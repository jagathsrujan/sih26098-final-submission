STHIRA-PGK — a de-spun canard guidance kit with an integrated multi-mode fuze
▸ full flight-function chart — slide 3

The problem

  • Dispersion grows with range — published record for the fielded class: 94 / 125 / 150 → 267 m CEP against the ≤ 30 m the requirement demands
  • Volume fire costs rounds, time on station, collateral exposure and battery time
  • YIL already manufactures the shell hardware — the missing capability is the kit
  • Retrofit only: fuze well + forward ogive — no shell re-qualification

The solution

  • Four fixed canards on a bearing-isolated de-spun ring — zero moving control surfaces
  • 0°/180° equal-cant pair: net guidance force = 2× one canard; roll torques cancel
  • 90°/−90° opposite-cant pair: net roll torque; zero net force
  • Ring roll angle aims the force vector — 2-D steering from one actuation axis
  • The alternator is the actuator (magnets on ring, coils on body) — no servos, gearboxes or linkages
  • No flight battery — the alternator powers the flight; supercapacitor buffer for motoring bursts; reserve battery for pre-launch and safe state only
  • Multi-mode fuze (proximity / time / impact) set over the existing AOP-22 inductive setter

Innovation and uniqueness

  • Loaded-generator sizing: 2.92 kW peak / ≈121 kJ per flight — derived sizing figures; the rig confirms them
  • Declared thermal path through the fuze well: ≥58 W/K needed vs 3–16 W/K bare thread — a designed interface; the rig closes it
  • The de-spun ring is the inertial platform — pickoff + magnetometer; spin-axis GNSS aperture
  • In-flight error estimator — bias estimation is established; the integration is ours
  • State-dependent authority gate — turns the apogee torque collapse into a designed constraint
  • Predicted-miss withhold: predicted miss > 150 m → function withheld, latched, dud-direction
